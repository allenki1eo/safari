The files in this directory are Copyright (C) 2009 Wildfire Games.

They are converted (glTF, compressed) from the 0 A.D. art at https://github.com/0ad/0ad:
lion, lioness, African elephant, giraffe, zebra, wildebeest, rhino, hippo
(hippopotamus), gazelle, crocodile (croc), elephant_calf (elephant_african_baby) and
giraffe_calf (giraffe_baby), and four used as stand-ins and recoloured in the game: hyena
(wolf, also drawn as the African wild dog), cheetah (tiger), buffalo (bull_a with the black
cattle texture) and warthog (boar). Clips were trimmed
to walk, run and idle and retimed to start at zero.
